# Quarterly returns

The table below sets out what each year returned, in the order the years
came, with the acceptance rate worked out to the nearest whole number.

| Year | Entries | Accepted | Rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | :---: |
| 2019 | 412 | 88 | 21% |
| 2020 | 503 | 104 | 21% |
| 2021 | 477 | 121 | 25% |
| 2022 | 551 | 139 | 25% |

Two things stand out. The first is that entries rose every year but one.
The second is that the rate held steady while they did.

| Note | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Counting | Entries are counted on the day they arrive. |
| Rounding | Rates are rounded to the nearest whole number. |
