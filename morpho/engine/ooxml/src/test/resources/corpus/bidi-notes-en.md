# Field notes from the Rabat workshop

Our localization workshop ran for three days at the مدينة الرباط venue,
and most sessions were held in both English and العربية الفصحى with live
interpretation for the visiting team.

## What we validated

- The phrase تحويل المستندات renders correctly inside an English sentence.
- Numbered Arabic items such as البند رقم 7 keep the digit on the right side.
- Bold mixed spans like **the قاموس entry** survive a full round trip.
